Kaiseki is the epitome of Japanese haute cuisine. Ours begins in Kyoto’s centuries-old traditions, then is refined with contemporary touches until colour, flavour, texture and presentation sit in one unhurried harmony. Nothing artificial reaches the plate: no MSG, no artificial colouring, no preservatives, and every drop of water purified by reverse osmosis.

To cut.
To cook.

Kappo means “to cut, to cook”. At TA.KE.EN, Xenri’s omakase counter, there is no menu to read. The chef chooses every course and composes it in front of you, a progression of colour, flavour, texture and presentation.
